# P2 figure-data dictionary

All paths in the manifest start at the project root. CSV uses UTF-8, decimal points and a header. The row count excludes the header. Explicit NA denotes an unavailable value or a conditional endpoint with no strict result. An observed count of zero remains zero. Energy uses the sealed convention E=−sᵀJs; regret uses the original V scale. The reference spin is 489 (zero-based) for the submitted 490-spin matrix.

## Hardware tasks and service returns

- `slot` is P01–P05 or F01–F20. `stage` is pilot or formal. `result_id` is the unique server-issued identifier. `returns` counts the curated solution set in one task. `return_index` is the original zero-based position within that set. Rows are clustered by task. Numerical bitstrings are in `evidence/p2/returns`.
- `task_margin.csv`, one row per slot. `strict_count` counts vectors with all forty legal chains. `energy_selected_index` minimizes (integer energy, return_index) among strict vectors. `min_energy` is its energy. `min_residual` and `max_residual` are the extrema of E+58722 in the returned set. `selected_margin=196−min_residual` and `set_min_margin=196−max_residual` are signed integer margins. `best_regret` minimizes original regret over strict returns. `energy_selected_regret` evaluates the energy-selected vector against the same frozen table. A margin of zero is outside the sufficient guarantee.
- `return_energy.csv`, one row per returned vector. `energy`, `residual`, `margin`, `strict`, `original_regret`, `candidate` and `energy_selected` respectively mean integer energy, excess over the mathematical ground value, distance below 196, all-chain legality, original V excess of the decoded candidate, its dictionary index and the per-task energy-selection flag. `locally_optimal_chains` counts chains attaining their own minimum q, including ties.
- `chain_detail.csv`, one row per (slot,return_index,chain). `chain` is one-based in plots, `group_size` counts its allowed candidates, `position` is the chosen wall location in [0,group_size−1], and `relative_position=position/(group_size−1)`. `candidate,A,B,X` identify the chosen row in its group. `q,q_min,q_excess` give integer cost, group minimum and their difference. `legal,optimal,critical` are binary flags for a legal group, group minimum including ties, and the group containing the unique global proxy winner. The aggregate winner minimizes (q,candidate) across chosen groups. For strict vectors the chain excesses times four sum exactly to the vector residual.
- `chain_optimal_250x40.csv` uses `slot,stage,return_index` as metadata and `chain_01...chain_40` as binary local-minimum indicators. `chain_task_25x40.csv` uses `slot,stage,returns` and the same forty columns, now counts from 0 to 10 within the curated set.
- `chain_count_distribution.csv` groups returned vectors by `stage,locally_optimal_chains`; `returned_count` is the number of vectors in that bin. `chain_position_identity.csv` groups by stage, chain, chosen position and candidate identity, with A/B labels. `returned_count` counts occurrences, `suboptimal_count` counts occurrences with positive group excess and `q_excess_sum` sums those excesses. Unvisited identities have no row. Full support is in the evidence dictionary and block maps.
- `chain_summary.csv` gives one row per stage and chain. `tasks,return_choices,local_optima` are counts; `mean_task_local_fraction` averages within-task local-optimum counts divided by returned set size with equal weight per task. `min_task_optima,median_task_optima,max_task_optima` describe those counts. These percentages describe fixed published sets within one group.
- `chain_association.csv` gives one descriptive association per task. `chain_choices` counts all vector-group decisions, `positive_excess_choices` the decisions exceeding their group minimum, `distinct_positive_candidates` their number of different identities. `most_frequent_suboptimal_ids` lists all tied modal identities; `max_identity_count` is their individual frequency. `position_excess_rho` is the Spearman correlation with average ranks for ties between relative position and integer excess among all 400 group choices; `position_excess_rho_positive` restricts to positive excess. Constant ranks give NA. `critical_optima` counts returned choices attaining the critical group's minimum. The same candidates and chains repeat and their order is fixed; no significance test or causal position effect is attached.
- `task_timeline.csv` gives the successful create acknowledgement's client timestamp `accepted_at_local`, the first completed poll's local observation `terminal_observed_at_local`, IANA `timezone`, `physical_start,physical_end` and `missing_reason`. Physical fields are unavailable. `timing_sources.json` lists `slot,source_path,sha256` for each original events file, ID-bound raw ACK and successful poll records. These local operational sources remain outside the portable release.

## Local simulated annealing

- `source` is H07 (historical, exploratory), fresh (registered seeds), or fresh_length_paired (view of the first two fresh blocks in both length arms). Views overlap; never sum them. `arm` is 40_real, 40_zero, or single_real. Zero retains constraints and uses the real q in the downstream decoder. `block,index` start at zero, with indices 0–99 in ascending order. `seed` is shared between the paired arms of the same cohort/block/index.
- `sa_attempts.csv` records every planned slot in the included cohorts. `status,failure` give outcome and exception class or NA. `strict` means all chains feasible, `partial` means at least one feasible (includes strict), `hit` means strict AND original_regret=0. `energy,candidate,original_regret` are integer energy and the selected candidate and its original regret whenever any chain is decodable. Filter on strict before reporting strict regret. `bits_hex` packs signs (+1 as 1) from left to right, MSB first; discard pad bits after `spins`. Unstarted/failed slots keep their planned denominator with hit=0. Empty bits are unavailable vectors.
- `sa_budget_blocks.csv` aggregates prefixes of length `budget` within an ordered block. `attempted,valid,failed` count started/valid/failed slots; `strict_returns` counts strict vectors; `any_strict,any_hit` indicate at least one strict vector or strict original hit. `first_hit_slot` is one-based within the block; NA means no hit in that prefix. `best_regret` is minimum strict original regret in the prefix. `energy_hit,energy_regret` use exactly one strict vector selected by minimum (E,index); with no strict vector the hit count is zero and regret NA.
- `sa_budget_curve.csv`, one row per source/arm/budget. `chains` counts groups; `planned_blocks,complete_blocks` count allocated blocks and blocks whose prefix actually started in full (including failed attempts). `attempted_slots,failed_slots` sum over blocks. `strict_hit_blocks,strict_hit_fraction` and `energy_hit_blocks,energy_hit_fraction` give the two hit definitions divided by planned blocks. `no_strict_blocks` gives the unconditional missing-strict count. `best_regret_median_conditional,energy_regret_median_conditional` are medians across blocks having a strict vector.
- `sa_paired_test.json`: `n,arms,endpoint,unit,classification,alpha,complete,assumptions` describe the preregistered comparison. Keys 11,10,01,00 of `table` refer in order to the business and zero-cost binary hit events in the first five attempts. `delta=(n10−n01)/20` is a signed fraction; `p_two_sided` is the exact conditional McNemar p-value under exchangeable discordance signs. Complete concordance yields p=1.
- `chain_ablation.csv` groups by `cohort,arm`. `planned,attempted,valid,strict,strict_hits` are restart counts. `conditional_median_regret` uses strict returns only. `spins,chains,shape,q_upper,penalty,T_initial,iterations_per_temperature,coefficient_max` identify matrix and annealing scale; `q_upper` is the real business scale even in zero-cost controls. `max_candidates_per_vector,max_classical_min_comparisons` describe classical aggregation load. `G1,G2,illegal_gap,ground_energy,matrix_hash,certificate_source` identify the legality and business certificates, positive minimum gap to illegality, integer ground energy, exact matrix bytes, and certificate provenance. Historical H02 uses endpoint padding and its own temperature rule; compare long and short on identical shaping/temperature using fresh_matched_2.
- `sa_sources.json` maps project-relative raw attempt, certification, dictionary and configuration paths to SHA-256. Per-slot local runs may require SDK licensing for regeneration. The hardware verification package uses only Python standard library.

## Manifest integrity

`figure_data_manifest.csv` records file path, identifier(s) of the target figure, non-header row count (for JSON, record count or one primary test), SHA-256, exact source paths and ordered source digests, generator and generator hash, reproduction command, evidence class, units, statistical denominator and verification status. For hardware rows, `evidence/p2/sha256.json` resolves the byte-level input list; for SA and client observations, the two source JSON indices resolve individual files. The manifest excludes itself. The closing Git revision anchors the manifest and this dictionary.
