# P2-A1 · Three orientations of the same oxynitride structure

Illustrative crystal structure of LaTiO₂N, adapted from Materials Project (mp-1222738). The Ti-centred network appears along c, along a and at an oblique angle in the same 2 × 2 × 1 supercell. The ball-and-stick control repeats the coordinates, centre and magnification. Only Ti–O and Ti–N bonds are displayed. Dataset DOI 10.17188/1745405, dataset record accessed 2026-11-28. VESTA 3.5.8 illustrative rendering, not DFT-relaxed in this study. The orientations are views of one structure, not independent evidence. This external structure served only for display and did not enter the surrogate, encoding or candidate selection.
